Volunteer

Developers

If you already have a contribution in mind you can find the project repositories on GitHub.

  • Get in contact via IRC-Chat #freenet @ irc.libera.chat, via FMS Forums, or via Sone
  • Report issues in the bug tracker hosted by MantisHub

To get into development right-away, have a look at one of the Hyphanet-Projects, check the starting issues in the bug tracker, or just get fred and fix something that annoys you.

Alternatively get the documentation of the Hyphanet Client Protocol and use Hyphanet as distributed backend for an external program.

If you want to tackle something that makes a big difference, have a look at the high-impact tasks.

We're happy to accept pull requests on GitHub as well as patches sent under a pseudonym through Hyphanet forums such as FMS.
